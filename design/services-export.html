OUR SERVICES.
Kelly Trucking supplies materials and dump-truck hauling for construction, landscaping, and site work. Alongside sister company Bakersfield Dirt, we help projects across Kern County and beyond source, import, and export what the job needs.
PREMIUM DIRT
SUPPLY SOLUTIONS
Kelly Trucking supplies dirt, clean fill, rock, base rock, gravel, sand, and asphalt for construction, landscaping, and gardening. Buy the material you need and coordinate delivery around your project.
EFFICIENT TRUCKING
SERVICES
Dump-truck hauling built around your job. Transfers, Super-10s, Bottom Dumps, and End Dumps help Kelly Trucking move materials efficiently, with clear communication from pickup through delivery.
CUT AND FILL
Match the material coming in with the excess going out. Kelly Trucking connects contractors with dirt sources and disposal locations, coordinating imports, exports, and hauling to keep cut-and-fill work moving.

HOW WE WORK.
QUALITY & VALUE
Materials suited to your job, consistent supply, and competitive pricing. We work with you to match the load and delivery plan to your project.
CLEAR COMMUNICATION
Personal service, honest expectations, and coordination from the first call through delivery. We build working relationships around your schedule and your crew.
SAFE & RESPONSIBLE WORK
Safety and environmental responsibility belong in the plan. We review site access, unloading conditions, and disposal needs before material moves.
