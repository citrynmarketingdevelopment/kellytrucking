DELIVERED.
Buy materials and get them where they need to go. Kelly Trucking supplies and hauls dirt, rock, gravel, sand, and asphalt, with cut-and-fill and site preparation services for projects across Kern County and beyond.
PREMIUM DIRT
SUPPLY SOLUTIONS
Kelly Trucking supplies dirt, clean fill, rock, base rock, gravel, sand, and asphalt for construction, landscaping, and gardening. Buy the material you need and coordinate delivery around your project.
EFFICIENT TRUCKING
SERVICES
Dump-truck hauling built around your job. Transfers, Super-10s, Bottom Dumps, and End Dumps help Kelly Trucking move materials efficiently, with clear communication from pickup through delivery.
CUT AND FILL
Match the material coming in with the excess going out. Kelly Trucking connects contractors with dirt sources and disposal locations, coordinating imports, exports, and hauling to keep cut-and-fill work moving.
COMPREHENSIVE
SITE PREPARATION
Get your site ready for the next phase. Coordinate grading, paving, leveling, and excavation with Kelly Trucking, with material supply and hauling planned around your project’s groundwork needs.
